## English

**Name:** Ludelo
**Tagline:** Your console, from afar.

**Short description (~300 chars):**
Ludelo turns your Windows 11 PC into a high-fidelity display for your PlayStation®4 or PlayStation®5. Play locally or remotely with your DualSense — adaptive triggers, haptics included — low latency, and a native Windows design.

**Full description:**
Ludelo is an independent, native remote-play client for Windows 11. Connect your PC to your PS4 or PS5 — over your home network or the internet — and enjoy your own games with an experience built for gamepads and big screens.

- Local and remote play with automatic console discovery
- Native DualSense support: adaptive triggers, haptics, touchpad, gyroscope
- Hardware-decoded H.264/H.265 video (NVIDIA, AMD, Intel)
- HDR and on-the-fly bitrate control from the in-game HUD
- Fully controller-navigable UI with native Windows 11 Mica effect
- One-time purchase: no subscriptions, no ads, no third-party accounts
- Open source (AGPL v3): fully auditable

Requires: Windows 11 (x64), your own PS4 or PS5 with Remote Play enabled, and a valid PlayStation Network account. This is independent software, not affiliated with or endorsed by Sony Interactive Entertainment. PlayStation, PS4, PS5 and DualSense are trademarks of Sony Interactive Entertainment.
